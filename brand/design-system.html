Measured contrast

Calculated in the browser from the real tokens, for both themes. Text pairs need 4.5:1 (WCAG AA). UI boundaries and icons need 3:1. Amber fills are decorative and always have a text label next to them.

Mobile rules
  • Capture button: 56px, center of the bottom bar, opens a bottom sheet.
  • Shared links arrive through the PWA Share Target and open the same sheet, prefilled.
  • Every touch target is at least 44px. The bottom bar respects the iPhone safe area.
  • Rediscover appears inside the feed, not as a push notification (opt-in later).
  • Filter chips scroll sideways and never wrap into 3 rows.
Components

Modal, sheet, menu, palette

Uses native <dialog>: focus stays inside, Esc closes, and focus returns to the button that opened it. Below 640px the modal becomes a bottom sheet. Menus support arrow keys.

Patterns

Empty, loading, error, offline

Every state tells you what is happening and what you can do next. Errors always say that your data is safe.

Foundations

Motion

CSS only. 120ms micro, 200ms standard, 320ms macro, all with ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Turn on "Reduced" at the top (or your OS setting): animations become instant and the indicators stay static with a text label.

Kept: the thread draws On save. 520ms thread, then the node springs in. Also used in the "Kept." toast.
Recalled: one pulse AI finished, or a memory resurfaced. Pulses once, never loops.
