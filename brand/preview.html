Anatomy — 48-unit grid

  • Back card (slate, +8°): everything else you've saved.
  • Front card with folded corner: the thing you captured.
  • Thread: Memento connecting it to other memories.
  • Amber node: the recalled memory. Always the accent, never recoloured.
  • Small mark drops the back card and thickens the thread so it survives 16px.
  • Thread + nodes take the background colour (paper / night), so the card reads as one solid shape.
